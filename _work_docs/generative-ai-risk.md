### The Problem
Risk event reporting was heavily bottlenecked by inconsistent data ingestion, manual documentation formatting, and non-standardized narrative submissions across teams.

### Core Solution
Developed an operational framework utilizing generative language models to streamline and standardize risk event reporting. Improved ingestion efficiency and reduced documentation bottlenecks for compliance operations.

### Capabilities & Impact
* **Operational Ingestion:** Drastically cut down documentation friction for operational risk teams.
* **Standardization:** Leveraged tailored prompt frameworks to turn raw incident descriptions into highly structured, compliant records.
